# Sand Beetle — Industrial Design Specification

## Function Analysis
Primary job: scoop granular material (sand, dirt), rotate body, dump into container.
Failure mode if primary job fails: bucket geometry doesn't retain material → product fails core play pattern.
Secondary: visually communicate "digger" instantly to a 3-year-old.

## Form Language Rationale
- **Shell body (oblate spheroid)**: Maximum interior volume for minimum visual weight. Rounded for child safety (no pinch points). Drop-test survivable at 1.2m.
- **Track pods (barrel cylinders)**: Round ends = no corners to catch on surfaces. Wide stance = tip-proof on uneven terrain (sandbox edges, carpet).
- **Boom arm (tapered box)**: Slight taper toward bucket = visual directional cue. Rounded corners (R8mm minimum) throughout.
- **Bucket (wide shallow U)**: Wide and shallow > deep and narrow — sand falls out cleanly on dump, better play feedback.

## Manufacturing Notes
- 3 main injection mold shots: shell top, shell bottom+track frame, arm assembly
- Snap-fit assembly (no screws for age 3+)
- Chrome rings: electroplated ABS insert, press-fit into arm joints
- Track rubber: TPR overmold on ABS track pod core

## Component Breakdown
1. **Shell**: oblate spheroid, split at equator for mold draft
2. **Track pod left/right**: mirrored, barrel cylinder with integrated wheel ridges
3. **Boom**: single tapered box, pivots at shell front
4. **Bucket**: wide U-profile, pivots at boom end
5. **Chrome joint rings**: 2× decorative rings at boom joints

# Sand Beetle — Concept Art Brief

## World Context
Made by a fictional toy company "BugBots Inc." for children aged 3–8.
Injection-molded ABS plastic, rounded for child safety (no sharp edges anywhere).
Sold at toy stores alongside Tonka and Bruder. Parents trust it; kids love it.
Emotional register: **joyful, chunky, indestructible**.

Real-world adjacents: Tonka classic dump truck, VW Beetle, John Deere toy tractor.

## Thumbnail Directions

**Direction A — "Full Bug"**
Premise: The machine IS a beetle, not inspired by one.
Silhouette: Six stumpy legs (not tracks), round carapace with spot markings, antennae bucket.
Material: Shiny hard plastic, ladybug red + black.
Mood: Playful.

**Direction B — "Happy Face"**
Premise: The machine has a face — headlights are eyes, bucket is the grin.
Silhouette: Wide low body, big round cab "head", arm extends from chin.
Material: Tonka yellow soft-matte ABS, big circular headlight decals.
Mood: Friendly.

**Direction C — "Pure Toy" (Selected)**
Premise: No face, no legs — just the purest rounded toy form that reads as digger.
Silhouette: Squashed ellipsoid shell, two barrel track pods, single chunky arm.
Material: Tonka yellow matte ABS + gloss black rubber tracks.
Mood: Nostalgic.

## Selected Direction: C — Pure Toy

### Primary Geometric Vocabulary
All forms derived from spheres and ellipsoids. No flat planes or sharp edges.
Shell: squashed oblate spheroid (wide, low, smooth).
Tracks: stubby cylinders with rounded ends.
Arm: tapered rounded box, bucket is a wide shallow U.

### Material Palette
1. Tonka Yellow ABS — matte, warm yellow (#F5A623), slightly chalky
2. Gloss Black Rubber — tracks only, slight sheen, very high roughness
3. Chrome Ring Accent — thin rings at arm joints, ABS + chrome plating

### Color Strategy
Value map: light (shell) dominates, dark (tracks) grounds it, chrome pops at joints.
Temperature: warm yellow body, cool chrome accents.
No more than 3 hues. Yellow is dominant at 70%, black 25%, chrome 5%.

# Concept Art Brief — EXP-02: Compact Spider
**Concept Art Director | Kids' Digger Simulator**

---

## World Context

The Compact Spider is produced by **Vex Robotics**, a near-future manufacturing house operating in the game universe's "new excavation" era — the period after track-based machines lost ground on unstable terrain like asteroid surfaces, sloped hillsides, and flooded construction sites. Vex's engineers looked at nature and saw the answer in arachnid locomotion: four independently articulating legs that can plant anywhere, level themselves, and step over obstacles while the digging mechanism stays perfectly stable.

**Who made it:** Vex is a startup spinout of a robotics research lab. Their aesthetic DNA is MIT Media Lab meets Boston Dynamics meets Kenaf industrial design — precise, functional, proudly exposing its mechanisms rather than hiding them. No sheet metal panels concealing hydraulics. The hydraulics *are* the aesthetic.

**For what purpose:** Specialist terrain excavation. This machine goes where the Classic Crawler cannot — steep slopes, rocky ground, rooftop construction. It is the "mountain goat" of the digger family.

**Relationship to user (child player):** Fascination through mechanical complexity. Where the Classic Crawler says "powerful," the Spider says "clever." Children who like robots, LEGO Technic, and machines-that-do-strange-things will love this one. The legs should feel animate — there should be a slight suggestion of creature-intelligence.

**Real-world visual adjacencies:**
1. Boston Dynamics Spot — functional robotic leg geometry, exposed actuators
2. LEGO Technic sets — coloured structural beams visible, mechanical transparency
3. Ghost in the Shell Tachikoma — wide body, articulated legs, friendly despite mechanical complexity

**Emotional tone:** Ingenious. Curious. Precise. This machine makes children ask "how does that work?" — and then they can see the answer because the mechanism is exposed.

## Thumbnail Directions

### Direction A — "The Technic Frame"
One-sentence premise: LEGO Technic structure language — visible frame beams, exposed axles, colourful structural differentiation.
Silhouette: Boxy central body with four visible beam-leg assemblies extending at cardinal points, arm mounted centrally.
Material one-liner: Structural beams in bright primary colours, ABS with visible fastener detail.
Mood word: **Playful**

### Direction B — "The Arachnid"
One-sentence premise: Direct spider geometry reference — narrow body slung between eight (reduced to four) bent legs.
Silhouette: Narrow central spine, legs bent at two joints each (femur/tibia), very wide footprint, low-hanging body.
Material one-liner: Dark matte carbon-look body, polished actuator joints.
Mood word: **Predatory**

### Direction C — "The Scout Mech"
One-sentence premise: Sci-fi robot walker — upright stance, visible hydraulic actuators on each leg joint, sensor dome on top.
Silhouette: Compact squared body raised on four crabbing legs, single central arm, sensor cluster at top.
Material one-liner: Brushed aluminium body with orange safety highlight on leg joints.
Mood word: **Heroic**

---

## Primary Geometric Vocabulary

Selected direction is a blend of A and C: LEGO Technic transparency with Scout Mech heroism.

- **Central body:** Compact, slightly hexagonal in plan — not a box, a chamfered octagon. Gives it a distinctive silhouette from any angle.
- **Legs:** Two joints per leg (hip and knee). Hip joint is a cylindrical swivel; knee joint is a hinge. Each leg terminates in a flat wide foot. The legs are tubular beams — circular cross-section, not flat plates.
- **Arm:** Single central excavator arm, same boom/stick/bucket structure as Classic Crawler but thinner and more precise. It emerges from the exact centre of the body roof.
- **Leg actuators:** Visible hydraulic cylinders on each leg joint. These are the most visually interesting element — they should be a different material (chrome/silver) to differentiate from the leg beams.
- **Foot pads:** Wide, flat discs (like a tripod foot). Communicates the stability function clearly.

Avoid: organic curves, rounded body forms. This machine is geometry-pure.

---

## Color Strategy

**Primary body:** Robot Orange — a distinctive departure from the Classic Crawler's yellow. Communicates: newer, more specialist, slightly dangerous.
- Reference hex: `#FF6B2B` — a warm, saturated orange. Not construction orange (too close to yellow). More vivid.

**Secondary structure:** Anthracite dark grey for leg beams and arm — creates strong contrast with orange body.
- Reference hex: `#2D2D2D`

**Accent:** Chrome silver on all actuator cylinders and joint pins — the sparkle of working mechanism.

**Value map strategy:** Orange body floats as a mid-value island; dark grey legs extend as low-value elements grounding it; chrome accents catch specular highlights and communicate mechanism. The overall read is dark machine with a glowing centre.
